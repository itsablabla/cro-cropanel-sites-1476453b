Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees surprise at checkout
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects are deterred when unexpected costs appear late in the purchase process, undermining trust.
Fine print hides extra costs
While fintech companies often boast about low or no maintenance fees, it’s crucial to read the fine print.
— optimus.tech
Prospects may not read terms carefully, leading to surprise fees and dissatisfaction.
Advertised price not final
Businesses use hidden fees to make prices appear lower at first glance, encouraging customers to buy before realizing the full cost.
— billshark.com
Customers expect transparency; hidden fees make comparison shopping difficult and erode trust.
Confusing cancellation and refund terms
The CANCELLATION AND REFUND clause outlines the terms under which a party may cancel an agreement and the conditions for receiving a refund.
— lawinsider.com
Unclear policies create anxiety about post-purchase flexibility, affecting buying decisions.
Return policy clarity matters
By keeping it simple and to the point, they ensure customers understand the key details of the return process upfront.
— websitepolicies.com
Clear return policies reduce perceived risk and increase purchase confidence.
